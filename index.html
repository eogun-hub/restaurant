About Us

The Flame and the Globe Welcome to Atlas & Ash, where primal fire meets refined global gastronomy. Founded on the belief that smoke and flame are humanity's most ancient and universal culinary languages, we bridge cultures through an ever-evolving tasting menu that transcends borders. At Atlas & Ash, every dish is an expedition—tracing the migratory paths of heirloom ingredients, ancient spice routes, and traditional open-fire cooking techniques from across the globe.

Our Philosophy: Fire as an Instrument

We reject the notion of borders in the kitchen. Instead, we use live fire—binchotan charcoal, aromatic woods, and smoldering embers—as a unifying canvas.

Global Heritage

Our recipes draw inspiration from the street-side robatayakis of Tokyo, the smoky parrillas of Buenos Aires, the clay-oven warmth of the Middle East, and the foraged hearths of Scandinavia.

Uncompromising Sourcing

We partner with independent farmers, ethical fishermen, and artisan purveyors who share our reverence for the land and sea.

The Tasting Journey

Every seasonal tasting menu is designed as a narrative arc, guiding guests through shifting textures, temperatures, and deep, complex smoke profiles.
